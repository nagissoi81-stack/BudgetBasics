Budgeting basics

Income

Money coming in: allowance, scholarship, internship or part-time pay.

Fixed expenses

Same amount every month, like hostel rent or a phone plan.

Variable expenses

Change each month: groceries, transport, outings.

Savings

Money set aside for goals and surprises. Pay it first, spend the rest.

Ask: Is it essential now?

Yes: prioritize it in this month's budget.

No: add it to a wish list and wait 48 hours. If you still want it, plan for it.
